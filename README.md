# Ender Radio

Ender Radio is a simple web-based radio player that lets you add any YouTube video and play just its audio. You paste a YouTube URL into the queue, and the app plays the sound without showing the video, making it perfect for music, podcasts, or any audio you want to listen to continuously.

## Install

1. Download the three project files: `index.html`, `style.css`, and `app.js`.
2. Place all three files in the same folder on your computer.

## Use

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari). You can double-click the file to open it directly.
2. In the "Add a song" section, paste a YouTube URL.  

   Supported formats:
   - `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID`
   - `https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID`
   - `https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VIDEO_ID`
   - `https://www.youtube.com/live/VIDEO_ID`
   - `https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID`

3. Click the **Add** button (or press Enter). The song will be added to your queue and start playing automatically if nothing is playing yet.
4. Add as many songs as you like. The radio will play them one after another in the order you added them.
5. Use the player controls to manage playback:
   - **Play/Pause** button to toggle audio
   - **Previous** and **Next** buttons to switch tracks
   - **Shuffle** button to randomize play order
   - **Seek bar** to jump to any point in the current track
   - **Volume slider** to adjust sound level

Your queue and song titles are saved in your browser, so they remain available the next time you open the app.

If a video cannot be played (for example, because it's region-blocked), the radio will automatically skip to the next song after a short delay.
